🃏 Court Piece — Privacy Policy

Single Rung / Court Piece multiplayer card game

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Summary

Court Piece is a real-time multiplayer card game played by sharing a room code with friends or family. It has no user accounts, shows no ads, and does not use analytics or tracking of any kind. This page explains exactly what little information the app touches and why.

What we store on your device

When you open Court Piece, your browser saves a few small values locally, using standard web localStorage:

  • A randomly generated player ID, so the game can reconnect you to your seat if your connection drops.
  • The display name you choose to type in (optional — you can leave it blank).
  • The avatar icon (an emoji, e.g. 🐯) you pick from a small built-in set.

This information stays on your device only. It is never uploaded to any database, never linked to your real identity, and is not accessible to us. Clearing your browser's site data for the app removes it completely.

What happens when you play

When you create or join a game room, your browser opens a live connection (via Socket.IO) to our game server so it can relay cards, moves, and chat-free game state between the players in that room in real time.

  • Room codes are short-lived and shared only with the people you choose to give them to.
  • Game state (whose turn it is, cards played, scores for the current match) lives in the server's memory only, for the duration of that room.
  • When a room empties out or a match ends, that in-memory state is discarded. We do not keep a persistent, server-side database of players, games, or results.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts — no email, password, or sign-in of any kind.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking — no Google Analytics, no ad SDKs, no fingerprinting.
  • No cookies used for tracking purposes.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties — there is essentially no personal data collected to sell in the first place.
  • No location access.

Children's privacy

Court Piece does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no accounts, emails, or identifying information are collected at all, there is nothing to gather regardless of a player's age. A chosen display name is stored only locally on the device and is never transmitted anywhere except to the other players temporarily present in the same game room.

Third parties

The app does not embed third-party advertising or analytics scripts. The only network communication it performs is the direct, real-time connection between your device and the Court Piece game server needed to play the game.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change (for example, if accounts or push notifications are added in the future), this page will be updated accordingly and the "last updated" date above will change.
